About

A practice the size of a person.

Sam Johns has been a licensed CPA since 2008, working first inside a regional firm and then on his own since 2014. The practice is intentionally small — most clients work with Sam directly, every year, for years.

The work is built around two ideas: that good books make tax season quiet, and that good tax planning makes the rest of the year quieter. Everything else is a consequence of those two.

"I'd rather have fifty clients I know by name than five hundred I don't."
